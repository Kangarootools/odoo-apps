Product Brand Schema

Show your product brands to Google & AI — automatically

Adds the schema.org brand to every product's structured data (JSON-LD), derived automatically from the product name. Helps your products appear in Google rich results and lets AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI) recognize your brands. Zero configuration, zero manual work.

Why you need it

  • Richer Google results with your product brand.
  • AI engines identify and cite your brands.
  • Better click-through and organic visibility.

How it works

  • Detects the brand from the product name (Xerox, Lenovo, Dell, HP, Huawei, Microsoft, Fortinet, and more).
  • Injects brand into the existing Product JSON-LD — non-destructively.
  • Every new product is covered instantly.

Safe & automatic

Fully defensive: if anything goes wrong, it returns the original markup unchanged — your pages never break. No settings to configure. Works on any Odoo eCommerce store. The brand list is easy to extend in the source.

Verify with Google's Rich Results Test after install — your Product data will include brand.
